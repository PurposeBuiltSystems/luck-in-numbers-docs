Privacy Policy for Luck In Numbers

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Luck In Numbers is operated by PurposeBuilt Systems, LLC. It tracks paper lottery tickets and optional shared pools. It does not sell tickets, take wagers, process payments, or improve the odds of a drawing.

Ticket data on your device Saved picks, confirmed ticket numbers, drawing dates, multipliers, result history, spending records, budgets, and tutorial preferences are stored on your device. Camera and photo-library access are used only when you choose to scan a ticket. Apple's Vision framework reads the image on your device. The image is used during confirmation and is not uploaded or retained in the saved ticket record. You may export a JSON backup to a location you choose; that file contains your ticket and spending data. Protect it as you would any personal record. Restoring a backup merges local records and does not restore pool identity.

Shared pools and anonymous identity Using a shared pool sends its name, host and member display names, ticket numbers, multipliers, recorded ticket costs, drawing date, timestamps and result recap to Google Firebase Firestore. Invite holders can read a pool and its browser preview. Members can append records while a round is open. A round locks at its drawing time or when the host locks it. Keep invite links private. A shared pool is an informal record, not proof of a purchase, ownership, or a legal agreement.

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous technical identity for pool actions. There is no signup form and no email or password is required. This identifier is linked to your pool membership and entries. We do not use it for advertising or tracking across apps. Losing this device identity can remove your ability to manage a pool; a ticket backup does not contain that credential.

Notifications If you use pool notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging stores a device registration token linked to your anonymous identity and pool membership, so the server can deliver results. These tokens are not visible to other pool members. Leaving a pool removes that device's registration for that pool. Notification permission is controlled in iOS Settings. Personal ticket checks run when the app opens and when iOS allows background refresh; background timing is not guaranteed.

Data sources and providers The app reads public lottery results from New York Open Data and jackpot data from our public feed. Requests may disclose your IP address to the service operator as part of ordinary network operation. Google Firebase provides pool storage, anonymous authentication and push delivery; Apple provides device notification delivery and on-device image recognition. We do not include advertising, purchases, subscriptions or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion Local records remain until removed from the device. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, but does not delete shared pools or exported backup files. Hosts can delete a shared pool in the app; its server-side notification registrations are then removed. Members can leave a pool, which removes local membership and the device's pool notification registration, while the shared historical entry remains visible to the group. To request removal of a display name or shared entry, contact us with the pool code and details sufficient to identify the record. Do not send an original ticket image or sensitive credentials. Shared records are retained for pool history until the host deletes the round or a verified deletion request is fulfilled.

Your choices You can use local ticket tracking without joining a pool, deny camera or photo access and enter numbers manually, disable notifications, export a backup, leave a pool, or ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. We do not sell personal information.

Age and responsible use The app is intended for adults 18 or older, or the applicable higher minimum age in your jurisdiction. Always verify results and prize amounts through official lottery channels. Historical statistics do not predict future drawings.

Contact and changes Email matthew@purposebuiltsystems.com with privacy or support requests. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
